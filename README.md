# Swiggy Business Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Swiggy food delivery data to uncover valuable business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, restaurant performance, food preferences, and geographical trends.

The analysis was performed using SQL for data extraction and business analysis, while Microsoft Excel was used to build an interactive dashboard for visualizing key metrics and trends.

The primary objective of this project is to help stakeholders understand customer ordering patterns, identify high-performing locations and restaurants, and make data-driven business decisions.

# Business Objectives

- Analyze overall business performance.
- Monitor revenue and order trends.
- Identify top-performing cities and states.
- Evaluate customer satisfaction through ratings.
- Understand food category preferences.
- Identify best-performing restaurants and dishes.
- Discover seasonal and weekly sales patterns.

#  Key Insights

## Revenue Insights

- Total revenue exceeded ₹53 Million.
- Average order value stood at ₹268.51.
- Revenue remained relatively stable throughout the year with noticeable peaks during high-demand periods.

## Customer Insights

- Average customer rating of 4.34 indicates strong customer satisfaction.
- More than 197K ratings were recorded, providing a reliable measure of customer experience.

## Food Preferences

- Non-Veg food contributed approximately 63% of total sales.
- Veg food contributed approximately 37% of total sales.
- Certain food categories consistently outperformed others in both orders and revenue.

## Geographical Insights

- Bengaluru emerged as the highest revenue-generating city.
- Several metropolitan cities dominated overall sales performance.
- Revenue distribution varied significantly across states.

## Restaurant Insights

- A small group of restaurants contributed a significant portion of total orders.
- High-performing restaurants maintained both strong ratings and order volumes.
